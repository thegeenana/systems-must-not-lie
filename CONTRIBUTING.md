# Contributing

Thoughtful challenges are welcome.

This repository is intended to develop testable engineering principles, not defend a fixed ideology. A useful contribution strengthens the connection between a system's claim, its authoritative truth and the evidence available to prove it.

## Ways to contribute

- challenge an existing principle with a concrete counterexample;
- improve the clarity or limits of a principle;
- propose a new principle;
- contribute a sanitized case study;
- improve the ADR template;
- identify an external claim that lacks an adequate proof obligation.

## Proposing a principle

A proposal should answer:

1. **Failure:** What goes wrong?
2. **Truth:** What invariant or promise must be preserved?
3. **Rule:** What engineering constraint follows?
4. **Example:** How does the rule affect a design?
5. **Proof:** What evidence demonstrates compliance?
6. **Challenge:** Where might the rule be incomplete or too costly?

Use plain language. Technology-specific examples are welcome, but the principle should explain why the example matters.

## Case studies

Case studies must be sanitized.

Do not include:

- customer or employer confidential information;
- proprietary source code;
- credentials, tokens or infrastructure secrets;
- personal data;
- claims that cannot be supported.

Describe the situation, competing constraints, decision, outcome and remaining uncertainty.

## Pull requests

Keep each pull request focused on one principle or one coherent improvement.

A pull request should:

- explain the problem;
- state the proposed change;
- identify affected principles or decisions;
- include diagrams only where they improve understanding;
- avoid formatting-only churn mixed with substantive changes.

## Conduct

Challenge ideas directly and respectfully. Assume good intent, distinguish evidence from preference and make uncertainty explicit.
